Privacy Policy

Money Manager: Expense Tracker

Effective October 19, 2026 Developer teamkernal Package com.teamkernal.expensemanager

In short

  • Nothing leaves your deviceAll expenses, budgets and categories stay in local storage.
  • One narrow network checkA check of the app's own Google Play page for required updates — nothing else uses the network.
  • No accounts, no adsNothing to sign in to, no tracking, no advertising.
  • You control your dataUninstalling the app permanently deletes everything.

1Overview

Money Manager: Expense Tracker ("the app") is a personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your financial data never leaves your device, and the only thing the app fetches over the network is its own public Google Play store page, to check whether a required update is available.

2Information we collect

We collect none of your personal or financial information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising network built into it. When it starts, the app loads its own public listing page on Google Play to check whether the installed version is still supported. The request contains no personal data, no device identifier, and no expense data, and it goes to Google, not to us: we never receive or log it. As with any web page you open, Google receives the technical details every web request carries, such as your IP address, under Google's Privacy Policy. If the check fails (for example, if you're offline), the app simply continues to work normally.

3Information you enter

When you use the app, you may enter:

  • Expense titles, amounts, dates, and categories you create
  • Monthly budget amounts
  • Your choice of light or dark theme
  • Your reminder preferences (on or off, how often, and what time)

This information is written directly to an SQLite database and local preferences file stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is never transmitted, synced, backed up to a server, or shared with the developer or any third party. We have no way to see it.

If your device's own OS-level backup (such as Android's auto-backup to your Google account) is enabled, your device manufacturer or Google may include this local data in that backup, the same as with any other app. That backup is controlled by your device settings, not by this app.

4Permissions

The app requests the Android INTERNET permission solely to perform the update check described above.

If you turn on expense reminders, the app also asks for permission to show notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), and uses RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED so your reminders still arrive after your phone restarts. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device by the operating system; they contain only a generic prompt to log your spending, never any of your expense data, and nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, or revoke the notification permission in your phone's settings.

The app requests no other permissions, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage outside its own sandbox, or any other device data.

5Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing service. The update check only reads the app's public Google Play page, as described above. It sends no information about you, your device, or your expenses.

6Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal or financial information from anyone at all.

7Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual expenses or categories within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its local database and preferences entirely and permanently — there is no cloud copy to remove separately.

8Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes in a way that involves data leaving your device beyond the update check described above (for example, an optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated first, the "Effective" date above will change, and the change will be described here in plain terms.

9Contact and support

For questions about this policy, help with the app, or any other query, email support.teamkernel@gmail.com.
